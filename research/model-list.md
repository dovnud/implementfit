# Compact and sub-compact models, 15–75 PTO hp

Checked 2026-11-14. A figure is **VERIFIED** only when this pass opened a manufacturer document and read that figure. Everything else is **UNVERIFIED**. No number from TractorData, FitTractor, a dealer listing, or a search snippet is copied in as a fact.

Horsepower labels are not interchangeable. Where a brochure prints both a horsepower figure and a kilowatt figure, both are shown. Mechanical horsepower times 0.7457 is approximately the kilowatt value. Metric PS divided by 1.36 is approximately the kilowatt value. Several John Deere sheets use one of those conversions for the engine cell and the other for the PTO cell. The notes say which.

The 15 PTO-hp floor is applied to verified PTO figures. The John Deere 1023E is listed anyway because its brochure puts it at 14.9 PTO hp, just under the floor, and buyers compare it with the 1025R.

This is not a complete US catalog. Brands with no opened spec sheet are listed at the bottom as gaps.

## What to do before any model page goes up

1. Open the PDF and read the cell. Text extraction merged at least two John Deere PTO cells badly enough that the kilowatt figure cannot be right.
2. Record engine standard and PTO standard separately.
3. Record gear and HST as separate PTO numbers when the sheet does.
4. Record the hitch phrase the sheet uses (“Limited Category 1,” “Category I,” “Category I & II”), not a normalized guess.
5. Put the document date and the URL in the page’s source line.
6. If the only copy is a dealer-hosted brochure, say so on the page.
